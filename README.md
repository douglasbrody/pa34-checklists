# PA-34-200 Checklists

Offline checklist viewer for iPad (also iPhone and desktop). Big tiles, text that resizes to fit the screen, Day / Night / Red modes.

**Install:** open https://douglasbrody.github.io/pa34-checklists/ in Safari on the iPad → Share → **Add to Home Screen** → open it once from the icon while online. After that it works in airplane mode (Edit shows "offline ready").

**Features:** normal, emergency and maneuver checklists; wind & runway entry for departure and arrival, shown as crosswind / headwind on the checklists (ATIS / tower winds are magnetic like the runway numbers; a METAR wind is true and is converted with the magnetic variation, 13° W at KBDR by default); takeoff data for the crew briefing; Hobbs / tach times; a scan reminder on emergency checklists.

**Preflight Planning:** weight & balance (N56307 empty weight 2,831.0 lb at 85.00 in; every station, both baggage areas, fuel, 3 gal taxi fuel, flight-time burn at 65 % / 75 %; kept from flight to flight until cleared, a blank entry counts as 0) with limits and a CG envelope; then the POH Section 8 charts (8-2 to 8-8, 8-13, 8-14) read for the entered temperature, pressure altitude (worked out from the altimeter setting and field elevation, or typed directly), weight and headwind, each shown with the reading drawn in red and labelled (temperature in °C and °F, pressure altitude, weight, wind, the values between panels and the answer) on a clean redraw of the traced chart, with the original POH page one tap away. A Range & endurance section gives, for the fuel after taxi at 45 / 55 / 65 / 75 % power: fuel flow, the manifold pressure to set at 2400 RPM (POH 8-16), true airspeed (POH 8-9), endurance and range (POH 8-10), each with and without the POH reserve of 45 min at 55 %, and ground miles with an average headwind or tailwind. The results fill the takeoff briefing (takeoff over 50 ft, accelerate-stop, single-engine climb and ceiling). Distances are rounded up and climb rates down; readings outside a chart are never extrapolated toward the unsafe side (no tailwind, nothing hotter, higher or heavier than the chart). The charts are traced in `tools/charts/*.json`; `node tools/build_charts.js` rebuilds `charts.js`, and `perf.js` is the reading engine.

**Checklist text format** (Edit › a checklist): `Item ... RESPONSE`, `# Section`, `! Caution`, `* highlighted`, `^ pulsing`, `| table | row |`, `{dep.sum}`-style flight-data tokens, `-> LABEL | subtitle : Checklist title` buttons, and `@HIDDEN`, `@BRANCH`, `@ATIS DEP` directives.

**Updating:** edit checklists in the app (Edit button), or regenerate `defaults.js` from the spreadsheet with `tools/convert_xlsx.py` (bump `DEFAULTS_VERSION` and add the previous version's signature to `KNOWN_SIGS`); then bump `VERSION` in `sw.js` (and `APP_VERSION` in `index.html`).
